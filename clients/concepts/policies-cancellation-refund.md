# Concepts Salon Spa — Cancellation & Refund Policy

**Effective: June 2026**

---

## BOOKING & CONFIRMATION

When you book an appointment with Concepts Salon Spa, your booking is confirmed once you receive a confirmation email or text message from us. At that time, your appointment is scheduled and a time slot is reserved for you.

For appointments involving a **deposit** (colour services, keratin treatments, extensions, and specialty treatments), payment is due at the time of booking to secure your reservation.

---

## CANCELLATION POLICY

### Cancellation Window

We require **48 hours' notice** to cancel or reschedule your appointment without penalty. A cancellation or reschedule request made less than 48 hours before your appointment time will be subject to a cancellation fee (see below).

**How to cancel:** Call us at [phone], text [text number], or use your online booking platform. Cancellations are effective when we receive and confirm them.

### Cancellation Fees

**If you cancel or reschedule with 48+ hours notice:** No charge. Your deposit (if any) will be refunded or credited to a future appointment, at your choice.

**If you cancel or reschedule with less than 48 hours notice:**
- A cancellation fee of **50% of the estimated service cost** is charged
- If a deposit was paid, the deposit is forfeited (and does not count toward the cancellation fee)
- Example: A $150 colour appointment cancelled 24 hours before = $75 cancellation fee

**No-show (you do not appear for your appointment):**
- Your full deposit is forfeited and non-refundable
- If no deposit was paid, a cancellation fee of **100% of the service cost** (or your first service price if estimated in advance) applies
- Example: You booked and paid a $150 deposit for a colour service and do not show up = your $150 deposit is lost

### Exception: Concepts Cancels

If Concepts needs to cancel your appointment due to illness, emergency, or scheduling conflict, we will contact you to reschedule at no penalty. If rescheduling is not available within 30 days of your original appointment date, any deposit paid will be refunded in full.

---

## REFUND POLICY

### Deposits

Deposits are **non-refundable if cancelled within the 48-hour window.** They are refundable if:
- You cancel with 48+ hours' notice (refund or credit to future appointment, your choice)
- Concepts cancels the appointment

Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.

### Service Refunds

If you are dissatisfied with a service, please notify us **within 48 hours of your appointment.** We offer:
- **Complimentary redo** (within 14 days) if the result is significantly different from the consultation or if an error occurred
- **Partial refund or credit** (at management discretion) if the service was compromised by circumstances beyond Concepts' control (e.g., a pre-existing hair condition you disclosed that made the service more challenging)

Refunds are **not offered** for:
- Results you requested that you later decided you don't like (unless we made an error in executing your specific request)
- Changes you requested during the service after the stylist began work
- Conditions you failed to disclose before the service (allergies, sensitivities, previous treatments)

To request a redo or refund, contact us at [phone] or [email] and describe the concern. Management will review and respond within 2 business days.

---

## PAYMENT METHODS & REFUNDS

We accept: Cash, Visa, Mastercard, Debit (Interac), Apple Pay, and Google Pay.

- **Credit/debit card refunds:** Processed to the card used at time of payment within 5 to 7 business days
- **Cash refunds:** Issued in person at the salon
- **Tips:** Tips added to a card payment can be refunded or adjusted if you contact us within 48 hours of the service

---

## LATE ARRIVAL

If you arrive more than **15 minutes late** for your appointment:
- Your appointment may be shortened to fit the remaining time, or rescheduled to another date at the stylist's discretion
- You will still be responsible for payment of services completed
- Rescheduling is subject to availability

We recommend arriving **10 minutes early** to complete any paperwork or consultations.

---

## DEPOSIT REQUIREMENTS & AMOUNTS

The following services require a deposit to secure your booking:
- **Colour services** (single process, balayage, highlights) — deposit = 50% of estimated service cost
- **Keratin treatments** — deposit = 50% of estimated service cost
- **Hair extensions** — deposit = 50% of estimated service cost
- **Specialty treatments** (lash services, brow lamination) — deposit = 50% of estimated service cost
- **Salon packages** — deposit = 50% of package cost

Deposits secure your appointment slot and are applied to your final bill on the day of service. Any deposit amount applied to your service is non-refundable once the service is completed.

---

## RESCHEDULING

You may reschedule your appointment **with 48+ hours' notice** at no charge, subject to stylist availability.

Rescheduling requests made **less than 48 hours before** your appointment are subject to a $25 administrative fee (or 50% of the service cost, whichever is greater) if the new date is within 14 days. If you reschedule to a date more than 14 days away, the fee is waived.

---

## COMMUNICATION & REMINDERS

We will send you a reminder email or text **48 hours** before your appointment. Please confirm your attendance or request a cancellation/reschedule at that time. If you do not respond to a reminder, your appointment will be held for you; we will not assume you are cancelling.
