# Concepts Salon Spa — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: June 2026**

## Introduction

Concepts Salon Spa ("we," "us," "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information when you visit our website, book appointments, or interact with us in any way.

We comply with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL).

---

## 1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

**When you book an appointment or contact us:**
- Name, phone number, email address
- Appointment history and service preferences
- Payment information (credit/debit card details — processed securely through our booking platform; we do not store full card numbers)
- Hair/skin type, allergies, and health information relevant to services you request
- Any special requests or notes

**When you visit our website:**
- IP address and browser information (via standard website analytics)
- Pages you visit and how long you stay
- Referring website or search query that brought you to us

**When you opt into communications:**
- Email or phone preferences for newsletters, promotions, and appointment reminders

---

## 2. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We use your information to:
- Schedule and manage your appointments
- Process payments and send invoices
- Communicate appointment reminders, confirmations, and service updates
- Respond to your inquiries and requests
- Improve our website and services (analytics only)
- Send marketing emails or SMS if you have opted in (see CASL section below)
- Comply with legal obligations (e.g., health and safety records)

We do **not** sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

---

## 3. WHO HAS ACCESS TO YOUR INFORMATION

Your information may be shared with:
- **Booking platform provider** (if using an online booking system like Vagaro, Square Appointments, or similar) — they process appointment and payment data on our behalf under a confidentiality agreement
- **Email service provider** (if subscribed to our newsletter) — they send emails on our behalf
- **Payment processor** (credit/debit card processor) — for secure payment processing
- **Staff members** at Concepts Salon Spa who need your information to deliver services or respond to inquiries

These third parties are contractually required to protect your information and use it only for the purposes we specify.

---

## 4. DATA RETENTION

- **Appointment records:** Retained for 7 years (for tax and service history purposes)
- **Payment information:** Retained only as long as necessary for billing and dispute resolution (typically 3 years under Canadian tax law)
- **Marketing communications:** Retained as long as you remain subscribed; you can unsubscribe anytime
- **Website analytics:** Retained for up to 2 years

Upon request, we can delete your personal information, except where we are legally required to retain it.

---

## 5. YOUR RIGHTS

Under PIPEDA, you have the right to:
- **Access:** Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
- **Correction:** Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information
- **Deletion:** Request deletion of your information (subject to legal retention requirements)
- **Opt-out:** Unsubscribe from marketing emails and promotional communications at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at [salon phone] or [salon email].

---

## 6. CASL COMPLIANCE (MARKETING EMAILS)

If you have opted into our marketing emails or SMS promotions, we comply with Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL). This means:
- You explicitly consented to receive marketing messages
- Every promotional email includes an unsubscribe link; you can opt out anytime by clicking it
- We do not send marketing emails to addresses you have not consented to
- All marketing communications are sent from a legitimate business email address

---

## 7. SECURITY

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. These include:
- Secure online booking platforms (SSL encryption)
- Password-protected staff access to client records
- Limited staff access on a need-to-know basis

However, no online transmission is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.
