MYSTEARY — REFUND & RETURN POLICY

ALL SALES ARE FINAL

mysTEAry operates a strict no refund, no return policy. We do not accept returns or offer refunds on any opened or used products. All digital gift cards and downloadable products are also non-refundable.

RECEIVING YOUR SHIPMENT

To protect yourself in the event of a damaged delivery, please follow these steps when your order arrives:

1. Before opening, record a single continuous, time-stamped video of the box and its full contents.
2. This video serves as your proof of damage and is required to receive a complimentary replacement.
3. If items arrive damaged, email your video along with your order number to nmartis@mysteary.com within 7 days of delivery. Your request will be reviewed and processed within 3–10 business days.
4. Please note: contacting us by phone will not expedite the replacement process.

INCORRECT ITEMS

If you received the wrong item, contact us within 7 days of delivery at nmartis@mysteary.com with a photo or short video and your order number. We will arrange a complimentary replacement within 3–10 business days of review.

GIFT ORDERS

If you received a mysTEAry product as a gift and it arrived damaged or incorrect, the gift recipient may contact us directly using the same process above.
